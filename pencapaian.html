Museum Wayang Educational Features
Exploration, Literacy & Cultural Achievements

This digital Museum Wayang is designed not merely as a collection storage place, but as an interactive educational platform. Through a digital approach, we present three main educational features to enrich cultural literacy in society, especially the younger generation:

1. Virtual Exhibition Hall

The Exhibition feature allows visitors to browse the museum corridors virtually. Each wayang collection is presented with zoomable detail, complete with bilingual audio explanations (Indonesian & English). Visitors can learn about the history, visual description, psychology/character, and lineage of each wayang figure.

2. Interactive Exploration & Scanning

Through the Explore feature, visitors physically at the museum can scan QR codes on each display case. The system will open in-depth information about the scanned wayang figure, ending with a short educational quiz to test the visitor's understanding of that character.

3. Achievement System (Gamification)

Untuk meningkatkan motivasi belajar, fitur Achievements mengadopsi sistem gamifikasi. Pengunjung akan diberikan lencana/gelar kehormatan secara otomatis (seperti 'Ki Dalang', 'Sang Waskita', atau 'Panglima Kaadilan') ketika mereka berhasil mencapai target edukasi tertentu, misalnya membaca dasar hukum pelestarian atau menyelesaikan sejumlah kuis.
